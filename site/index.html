PhysiqueLapse

See your progress, lined up.

Take your progress photo with a faded ghost of the last one over the camera, so every day lines up. PhysiqueLapse sorts photos by pose, aligns your shoulders, elbows and hips onto one reference, and plays them back as a time-lapse.

Open the app Windows app

Invite only: ask the person who runs your server for an invite code.

Ghost camera

A see-through ghost of your reference or yesterday's photo over the live camera, with a live pose match score and auto-capture.

Aligned time-lapse

Every photo of a pose is scaled and rotated onto the same reference, so only you change between frames.

Daily streaks

One photo a day, a streak counter, and a one-tap flipbook of the whole series.

Private by design

Your photos live on a private server in your own library. No ads, no analytics, no AI services; all processing happens on the server.

Getting started

  1. Open the invite link you were sent and create your account.
  2. Add it to your home screen: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone), or Chrome → Install app (Android).
  3. Capture: pick a pose or start a daily series, line up with the ghost, shoot. Photos can be saved to your camera roll too.
